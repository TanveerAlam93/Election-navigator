==========================================
       UNITED STATES ELECTION PROCESS
==========================================

1. REGISTRATION
   - You must be 18 years old and a US Citizen.
   - Deadlines vary by state (usually 15-30 days before election).
   - Some states allow "Same Day Registration."

2. THE PRIMARIES (January – June)
   - Voters choose which candidate will represent their party (Democrat or Republican).

3. THE GENERAL ELECTION (November)
   - Held the first Tuesday after the first Monday in November.
   - You are voting for "Electors" who represent your state.

4. THE ELECTORAL COLLEGE
   - There are 538 total electors.
   - A candidate needs 270 to win.
   - Most states are "winner-take-all."

5. INAUGURATION (January 20)
   - The winning President is officially sworn into office.
